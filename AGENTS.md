# Repository Instructions

Before taking any action in this repository, read `rules.md` in full and follow it.

Re-read `rules.md` at the start of every new task and before resuming work after an interruption or
context change. Its accounting integrity, validation, immutability, auditability, deterministic
calculation, AI separation, domain modeling, and scope constraints are mandatory.

At the end of every task, update `memory.md` with a concise dated record of the work and its
verification status. Do this even when the task was read-only or documentation-only. Treat that
memory update as the task's final bookkeeping step; it does not recursively require another entry.
